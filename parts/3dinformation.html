    Rules

    Three things to remember

    One style per scene

    • Do not mix low-poly and realistic models in one scene.
    • HDRIs are the exception. A Poly Haven HDRI fits every style.
    • For realistic characters, use Poly Haven and ambientCG.
    • For cartoon scenes, use Quaternius characters.

    Credits on YouTube

    • CC0: no credit is necessary.
    • CC-BY / OGA-BY: put a credit in the description.
    • CC-BY-SA / GPL: do not use in a monetized video.
    "Guy" by CLOAKING .US poly.pizza/m/0eU7bl0a6Cg CC BY 3.0 — modified

    Animations from other sources

    • Humanoid animations can be retargeted from one rig to another.
    • Quaternius uses a standard humanoid rig. Animations from other common rigs fit it.
    • KayKit and Kenney characters look best with their own animations.
    • An auto-rig tool can rig a mesh with no rig and human proportions.
    • Big-head, small-body characters make retargeted animations clip.
